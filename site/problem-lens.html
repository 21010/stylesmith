Problem Lens

Stop hovering over squiggly lines or toggling the problems panel. Stylesmith renders errors and warnings inline, right next to your code, giving you immediate feedback.

1 | function calculateTotal(items) {
2 |   const total = items.reduce((a, b) => a + b);
3 |   reutrn total;
Cannot find name 'reutrn'. Did you mean 'return'?

4 | }

Zero Friction

By rendering diagnostics inline, you see typos and logical errors immediately. You do not need to open the terminal or move your mouse to discover what broke.

Fully Integrated

Problem Lens uses the diagnostic colors of your active palette. Errors, warnings, and hints match the current theme instead of clashing with default editor colors.
