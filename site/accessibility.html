Accessibility Statement

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Here is what we test, what we haven’t tested yet, and how to tell us about a problem.

What we test

  • Every change is checked automatically. On every pull request and every change to the main branch, axe-core runs the WCAG 2.2 level A and AA checks on every page, on a wide screen and on a phone.
  • Keyboard. Every link and button can be reached with the keyboard, shows where the focus is, and is never hidden under the header. Every page has a “Skip to content” link.
  • Motion. Everything that moves by itself (the videos, the carousels and the blinking cursor) can be paused with the button in the header. It starts paused when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Contrast. The site’s colors are tested for at least 7:1 contrast for body text and 4.5:1 for everything else.
  • Small screens. Every page works down to 320 pixels wide without scrolling sideways.

Not tested yet

Automated checks find only part of what WCAG asks for. We haven’t yet tested the site with a screen reader (such as NVDA or VoiceOver), at 200% zoom, or with custom text spacing, so we don’t claim full conformance yet.

The videos of the effects are silent demonstrations; the text next to each one describes what it shows.

The extension

This statement is about the website. For how the Stylesmith color themes are tested for contrast, see Ergonomics.

Report a problem

If something on this site is hard to use with your setup, please open an issue on GitHub and tell us the page, what you tried, and what happened.
