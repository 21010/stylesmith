Ergonomics First

Themes are more than aesthetics; they define your workspace environment. Here is how Stylesmith enforces strict accessibility standards, guarantees legibility, and actively prevents eye strain.

Core Pillars

  • WCAG 2.1 Standards
  • Eye Strain Protection
  • Color-Vision Deficiencies

WCAG 2.1 Standards

The Challenge

Many custom themes ignore contrast ratios entirely, leading to gray-on-gray syntax highlighting that becomes illegible in bright rooms or on low-brightness monitors.

Our Approach

Every palette is tested against WCAG 2.1 guidelines. We enforce strict minimum contrast ratios (AA and AAA) for all critical syntax tokens against their background.

Why It Matters

Your code remains perfectly readable regardless of ambient lighting conditions or screen hardware, preventing squinting and posture deterioration.

Eye Strain Protection

The Challenge

While low contrast is bad, extreme high contrast (like pure #FFFFFF text on a pure #000000 background) causes text glare and halo effects, which rapidly tires the eyes.

Our Approach

We enforce a strict maximum contrast ratio cap. Backgrounds are slightly lifted, and text is gently dimmed to neutralize halation.

Why It Matters

It eliminates visual glare and drastically reduces optical fatigue, allowing for much longer, more comfortable coding sessions.

Color-Vision Deficiencies

The Challenge

Themes that rely solely on slight hue variations for syntax highlighting (e.g., red errors vs green strings) often become indistinguishable for developers with color-vision deficiencies (CVD).

Our Approach

We compute precise Delta-E color differences across our palettes, ensuring varying luminance and saturation, rather than just hue, separate critical UI elements.

Why It Matters

Diagnostic errors, warnings, and syntax keywords remain clearly distinguishable to all developers, preventing simple visual mistakes.
