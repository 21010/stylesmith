// Mathematically Ergonomic

Our themes aren't just "thrown together" in a color picker. Every pixel is verified by our automated test suite against strict WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards. We enforce mathematical minimums for readability (AA and AAA contrast ratios), strict mathematical differences to support color-vision deficiencies (color blindness), and a maximum contrast ratio cap to eliminate text glare and reduce long-term eye strain.
